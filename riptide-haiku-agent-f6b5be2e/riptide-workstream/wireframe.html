Key Features

1. Mode Toggle: "Scientific" button shows/hides advanced functions without interrupting your calculation.

2. Operator Precedence: All expressions now respect PEMDAS. Multiplication and division happen before addition and subtraction. Exponentiation is evaluated right-to-left (2^3^2 = 512, not 64).

3. Parentheses: Group sub-expressions with ( and ). Nested parentheses work: ((1+2)×3)+4 = 13.

4. Trig Functions: sin, cos, tan, arcsin, arccos, arctan. Toggle between degrees (default) and radians with the DEG/RAD button. Example: sin(90°) = 1, but sin(π/2 rad) = 1.

5. Logarithms: ln (natural), log₁₀ (base 10), and eˣ (exponential). Example: ln(e) = 1, log₁₀(100) = 2.

6. Powers & Roots: x², x³, √x (square root), ∛x (cube root), and x^y (arbitrary exponent). Example: 2^10 = 1024, 27^(1/3) = 3.

7. Constants: π (pi) and e inserted as needed. Example: sin(π/2) = 1.

8. Full Expression Display: See your entire calculation as you type, not just the pending operation.

9. Keyboard Shortcuts: Use mnemonics (s=sin, l=ln, p=π) plus all existing controls (Esc=clear, Backspace=delete, Enter=equals).
